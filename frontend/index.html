Antigravity agent architecture powered by mathematical cognitive modeling, psychometrics, and reinforcement learning.

1. Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT)

Models hidden student mastery $P(L_t)$ per concept through a 2-state Hidden Markov Model:

P(L_t | Correct) = [P(L_t)(1-P_S)] / [P(L_t)(1-P_S) + (1-P(L_t))P_G] P(L_{t+1}) = P(L_t | Obs) + (1 - P(L_t | Obs)) * P_T
  • P(T) Learn rate: 0.20
  • P(G) Guess rate: 0.20
  • P(S) Slip rate: 0.10

2. Item Response Theory (2PL IRT)

Estimates latent student ability $\theta \in [-4, +4]$ and item discrimination $a$ / difficulty $b$:

P(\theta) = 1 / [1 + exp(-a(\theta - b))] \Delta \theta = \alpha \cdot a \cdot (y - P(\theta))
  • Item discrimination $a \in [1.0, 1.8]$
  • Calibrated item difficulty $b \in [-2.0, +2.0]$

3. Fisher-Information CAT Selection

Picks questions maximizing information gain at the student's current ability boundary:

I(\theta) = a^2 \cdot P(\theta) \cdot (1 - P(\theta)) Score(q) = w_1 I(\theta) + w_2 Weakness + w_3 Novelty - w_4 Recency
  • Maximum informativeness when $b = \theta$
  • Enforces 2–3 attempt cooldown on misses

4. Contextual & $\epsilon$-Greedy Bandits

Balances exploration of unpracticed concept tiers with exploitation of high-leverage learning gains:

LinUCB: a^* = \arg\max (\hat{\theta}_a^T x + \alpha \sqrt{x^T A_a^{-1} x})
  • Decaying $\epsilon$-greedy from 15% to 5%
  • Features: mastery, ability $\theta$, accuracy, time-delta
