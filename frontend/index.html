1. Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT)

Models student mastery probability $P(L_t)$ per concept through a 2-state Hidden Markov Model with online Bayesian updating:

P(L_t | Correct) = [P(L_t)(1-P_S)] / [P(L_t)(1-P_S) + (1-P(L_t))P_G] P(L_{t+1}) = P(L_t | Obs) + (1 - P(L_t | Obs)) * P_T

2. Item Response Theory (2PL IRT)

Estimates latent psychometric ability $\theta \in [-4, +4]$ and item parameters $(a, b)$:

P(\theta) = 1 / [1 + exp(-a(\theta - b))] \Delta \theta = \alpha \cdot a \cdot (y - P(\theta))

3. Fisher-Information CAT Selection

Selects questions maximizing information gain at the student ability frontier:

I(\theta) = a^2 \cdot P(\theta) \cdot (1 - P(\theta)) Score(q) = w_1 I(\theta) + w_2 Weakness + w_3 Novelty - w_4 Recency
